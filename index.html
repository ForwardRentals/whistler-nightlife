Earn the day. Own the night.

Whistler's nightlife starts the moment the lifts slow down. Here's how a night in the Village plays out, where to go, and how to fit it around a big day in the mountains.

How a night goes

A Whistler night, hour by hour.

3 pm

Après

Lifts wind down and the base-area patios fill. Ski boots are standard dress.

6 pm

Dinner

The Village has everything from fast pizza to fine dining. Book ahead on weekends and holidays.

9 pm

Warm up

Pubs and lounges get busy, and live music starts in a few of them.

11 pm

Late

The clubs open up. Expect lineups on Saturdays and holiday weeks.

Know before you go

Tips for a good night.

  • Bring ID. The drinking age in BC is 19, and two pieces of ID are often requested at the door.
  • Plan your ride home. Whistler Transit runs local buses, taxis are busy at closing, and the Village is very walkable.
  • Never drive the Sea to Sky after drinking. Highway 99 is unforgiving. Stay over or arrange a ride.
  • Expect lines on big weekends. Holidays, Crankworx and long weekends are busiest. Go early.
  • Dress for the walk. Village nights are cold most of the year, even in summer.
